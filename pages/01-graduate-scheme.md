# What is STFC?

**Science and Technology Facilities Council**

- Part of **UKRI**, government funded
- Runs national labs, including:
  - **Daresbury Laboratory** - Warrington, where I work
  - **Rutherford Appleton Laboratory** - near Oxford
- Large-scale science: ISIS Neutron and Muon Source, Central Laser Facility, Hartree Centre
- Works with **CERN**, **ESO**, **ESS** and **NASA**

# What is the Graduate Scheme?

- Two year scheme
- Several projects that can last 3, 6 or 12 months each
- Training:
  - Insights
  - Belbin
  - Residential

# Why Work at STFC?

- Cool Projects - you are working on code that supports world-leading research
- Time Off - 30 days holiday + 8 bank holidays + 2.5 privilege days
- Good Pay - graduates are paid just under £40K (Band C) + really good pension
- Flexible Working - most graduates do around 2-3 days in the office
- Takes Care of Employees - large union presence and free travel, food and hotels when necessary
